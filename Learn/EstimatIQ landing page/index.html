What is EstimatIQ?

EstimatIQ is an AI-powered project estimation platform built for sales teams. Paste in a client's project description and our engine returns a detailed, scope-aware estimate — broken down by phase, hours, and cost — in under 10 seconds. No spreadsheets. No back-and-forth with engineering.

Who Is It For?

  • Sales engineers closing complex technical deals
  • Account executives who need fast, credible quotes
  • Agencies and consultancies scoping client projects
  • Pre-sales teams at SaaS and professional-services companies
  • Freelancers and small studios estimating custom work

How It Works

  1. 1

    Describe

    Paste or type your client's project description into EstimatIQ. Plain language works perfectly — no structured input or templates required.

  2. 2

    Analyze

    Our AI engine breaks the description into discrete workstreams, assigns complexity levels, and calculates hours and costs per phase using your team's own rate card.

  3. 3

    Review & Export

    View the structured estimate, adjust any line items inline, then export to PDF, Excel, or push directly to your CRM — ready to send to the client.

Key Features

AI-Powered Scoping

Custom Rate Cards

Upload your team's hourly rates and EstimatIQ applies them automatically to every phase, role, and deliverable.

Export Anywhere

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are EstimatIQ's estimates?
Our estimates are accurate within ±15% for most standard project types. Accuracy improves the more context you provide — team size, technology stack, and known constraints all help the AI scope more precisely.
Can I use my own billing rates?
Yes. You can upload a custom rate card for any number of roles (designer, developer, PM, QA, etc.) and EstimatIQ will apply your rates to every future estimate automatically.
Does EstimatIQ integrate with my CRM?
We support native integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive on the Growth and Enterprise plans. Our REST API lets you connect any other system on the Enterprise plan.
Is my data secure?
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We are SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR-compliant. We never use your project data to train our models without explicit opt-in.
